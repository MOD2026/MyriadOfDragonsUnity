# Human Playtest Scripts v1

**READY FOR CC — Yes / No:** Approve these three short owner playtests as the human-feel gate before further economy or UX work?

**How to record:** one sentence per checkbox is enough. Capture a screenshot only when something is confusing, blocked, or unexpectedly satisfying. Do not retry through developer tools or change values during a run.

## 1. Cold start — five minutes

**Route:** Home → Campaign → 1-1 → 1-2 → 1-3 → Collection burn/Evolution surface.

- [ ] Start from a fresh profile. On Home, identify the next obvious action within a few seconds.
- [ ] Open Campaign and select 1-1. Note whether the entry cost, deck requirement, and launch action are clear before committing.
- [ ] Complete 1-1, return to Campaign, then repeat for 1-2 and 1-3 as they unlock.
- [ ] After each result, say what you believe unlocked, what reward mattered, and what you should do next.
- [ ] Open Collection. If a surplus card exists, attempt one burn and then inspect Evolution. If no legal burn/evolution is available, record that exact blocker; do not manufacture one.
- [ ] Note: did the game explain why a duplicate, material, or Gold is missing without making you feel lost?

**Zihan notes:** first point of confusion; any action that looked tappable but was not; whether the campaign goal felt like “one more stage” or “I have no idea.”

## 2. AI-spell feel — five minutes

**Route:** enter a normal Campaign fight with a confirmed deck → Auto Formation or manual formation → Start Battle → watch combat and cast available player spells.

- [ ] Before combat begins, identify which choices are yours and which combat actions are automatic.
- [ ] Watch at least one AI spell event and one player spell opportunity.
- [ ] Cast a player spell when it feels useful; do not wait for a perfect test case.
- [ ] Finish or exit the encounter normally.

**Gut-check questions:**

- Did the AI spell feel like a fair opposing decision or a surprise that erased agency?
- Could you tell what happened and why, without reading logs?
- Did your player spell feel timely and meaningful?
- Did automatic combat still leave you with enough strategic ownership?

## 3. Shop pack → burn → evolve — five minutes

**Route:** Shop → purchase one available card pack → Collection → inspect the new card/duplicate → burn only a genuine surplus → inspect Evolution.

- [ ] Before purchase, state what you expect the pack to give and what it costs.
- [ ] Purchase one pack through the normal player route; verify the result appears in Collection.
- [ ] If the result is a surplus, choose one burn outcome deliberately. If it is not a surplus, record that and stop the burn branch.
- [ ] Open Evolution for a card you would actually want to improve. Read each missing requirement.
- [ ] Do not spend around a missing requirement just to complete the script.

**Scarcity-feel questions:**

- Did a duplicate feel disappointing, useful, or both?
- Is it clear that burn materials support progression but do not replace the right duplicate/other requirements?
- Did the next Evolution goal feel earned and understandable, rather than arbitrary or paywalled?
- After the pack, do you want to plan the next collection decision, or did the loop feel pointless?

## End-of-session report

Send only:

1. The single biggest confusion.
2. The single most satisfying moment.
3. One screenshot, if there was a blocker.
4. `Go` / `Hold` for each route: Cold Start, AI Spells, Shop→Collection.
