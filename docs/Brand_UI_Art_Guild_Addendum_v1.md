# Myriad of Dragons — Brand, UI & Art Guild Addendum v1

**Status:** Approved extension under MOS v1.2. This addendum fills the local-project gap where the
referenced UI/UX Bible and brand/art bibles are not present as readable repository files.

## Brand intent

Guild competition should feel like public heraldry and earned command, not a generic leaderboard
or casino reward layer. Visual status communicates history, service and achievement while staying
inside the established dark-fantasy realm/capital direction.

Core qualities:

- sovereign, ceremonial and martial;
- painterly dark-fantasy materials rather than glossy sci-fi panels;
- readable at mobile landscape scale;
- prestigious without resembling a paid VIP ladder;
- one coherent visual family across guild, rank, league, office and Hall-of-Fame assets.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Guild identity: emblem, name and league.
2. Current event: season score, rank and remaining time.
3. Player context: personal rank, contribution and eligible rewards.
4. Cooperation: donation, help and research calls to action.
5. Command: R4/R5 controls shown only when authorised.

Power is never the only visible status. Contribution, combat achievement, building, research,
event participation and lifetime achievements receive parallel presentation.

## Asset families required

- Five league emblems: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Dragon.
- Five R1–R5 rank marks with distinct silhouettes.
- Five appointed-office marks: Master Builder, Royal Scholar, Quartermaster, First Envoy and
  Guild Champion.
- Rank 1, top 3 and top 10 frame variants in one family.
- Guild emblem base, editable heraldic layers and fallback/default emblem.
- Donation, Help, Research, Store, Rankings, Hall of Fame, Announcement and Event icons.
- Guild Champion animated frame plus motion-disabled static equivalent.
- Promotion, relegation, milestone and reward-claim presentation assets.

## UI rules

- Guild is one Home destination with internal subnavigation; do not create a Home button for every
  guild subsystem.
- All names, ranks, points, timers, percentages and reward quantities are runtime text.
- Rank and league states use icon, label and shape; colour alone is insufficient.
- The player's own row remains anchored or easy to locate even when outside the displayed top list.
- Leaderboards show scoring period, eligibility, tie-break rule and reward band before the player
  commits effort.
- Power Showcase is explicitly labelled as prestige-only.
- R4/R5 controls show consequence and scope. Ownership transfer, research cancellation, office
  revocation and disbanding require confirmation; disbanding also shows the 24-hour pending state.
- Temporary office screens show eligibility, holder, benefit, expiry and appointment audit state.

## Reward presentation

- Milestone rewards are visually primary; leaderboard placement is secondary.
- Top functional reward bands share the same reward chest treatment.
- First place receives stronger heraldry, frame animation and title treatment rather than a much
  larger material chest.
- Repeat champions gain Hall-of-Fame stars or frame evolution without multiplying economic value.
- Do not use slot-machine reels, misleading near-miss effects or excessive confetti for ordinary
  contribution and ranking changes.

## Accessibility and localisation

- Minimum icon variants: full-colour, monochrome, selected and disabled.
- Every emblem and badge requires a short accessible label.
- Do not embed English rank names, numbers or timers in generated art.
- Motion-reduced mode preserves all meaning and replaces ceremonial animation with a static state.
- Small leaderboard rows must remain legible under text expansion and non-Latin scripts.

## Handover rule

Any future UI/UX Bible, Brand Bible or Art Bible master must incorporate this addendum or explicitly
record a superseding decision. Until those master files are available locally, this repository
addendum is the readable authority for guild competition visuals.
